    Tileset Studio

    Flow: Open tileset (PNG) → lay models over the art → Process → check the output → Export. Axes: X across (east-west), Y depth (north-south on the ground, bigger = nearer you), Z height (up). The view is the tileset's own projection: a point (X, Y, Z) shows at canvas (X, Y − Z), so going deeper moves a point down the screen and rising moves it up; the axis key at the bottom right of the view shows the directions. Model what the art shows: fronts stand on the floor line, tops sit at their height. Holes (art no model covers) show in magenta.

    More tools (each panel explains itself; hover any control): Pencil (P) draws a custom-shaped object, on a model's face or in empty space. File ▸ Import 3D / stamp adds models from an OBJ or another project, or stamps them over your art. Export ▸ 3D models (OBJ) writes mesh geometry for other 3D tools. References (left panel) lay another tileset over yours as a ghost. README.md has the full manual.

    Pen / touch: the left palette has every tool; palette tools are "armed" (press where to start, drag, let go). The bar at the bottom gives X/Y/Z locks, an exact value, "fine", apply and cancel. Two fingers pan and pinch-zoom; the top-right panel pans, zooms, looks round and frames. « / » at the top of either panel folds it away.

    Scaling snaps sizes to whole steps; a part smaller than the step (a thin leg) scales in the largest power-of-two step that fits it.

    Output view: drag to move the light, Shift+wheel for its height; wheel (or − / +) zooms, middle/right/Space-drag pans, ⛶ fits. Hover reads the pixel; double-click selects its model; "next hole ›" visits uncovered spots; "⚠ Tile problems" lists cells the importer will misread (click it for the Pieces view). Floor shows the floor plan from above: dark stripes = open underneath (a top on legs), light stripes = a gap between stacked parts.

    Into Godot: set the Name at the top of the Export menu, then Export ▾ → Godot bundle: one name.export.json with the art, maps and collision. Copy it into the Godot project; the Studio Import plugin unpacks it and builds name_tileset.tres by itself (and again whenever you export over it).

    Separate files: name_height.png (R height px/255; G/B highlights if packed), name_normal.png (OpenGL: R east, G up, B toward the viewer), name_pbr.png (R roughness, G metallic), name_footprints.json (per model: floor footprint, height range, collision).
